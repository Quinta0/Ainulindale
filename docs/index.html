questions

how do i update slskd or explo?
Change SLSKD_VERSION or EXPLO_VERSION, restart.
what if listenbrainz is down?
It retries next night; the mixes run on cached lookups.
why must navidrome be an ip?
All traffic leaves through the VPN firewall, opened for exactly that address.
which machines?
amd64 and arm64. Unraid works as is.
